Quiznetic Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiznetic collects, uses, and stores
information when you use the app, including the Web version hosted at
quiznetic.eirenya.com.

Quiznetic is a personal project operated by Steve Alomassor, an individual
based in France, who is the controller of the personal data processed for this
service. Quiznetic is the product name, not a separate legal entity.
Contact for privacy questions and rights requests: quizneticapp@gmail.com.

1) Information We Collect
- Account information from Firebase Authentication:
  - user ID (UID)
  - email address (if provided by your sign-in method)
  - display name and provider profile information made available to Firebase
    Authentication, including a profile image where the provider supplies one
  - anonymous account status for guest users
  - profile creation and last-seen timestamps
- Gameplay and score data:
  - category, difficulty, score, and timestamps
  - leaderboard entries and rank-related fields
  - score attempt identifiers for idempotent submission
- If you contact support: your contact address, request and necessary replies.
- Technical request information needed to deliver and secure the Web app, such
  as IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time.
- If you accept optional Web Analytics: browser/cookie identifiers, visits,
  screen views and quiz activity, including category, difficulty, question count,
  score and score-submission outcome. Google may also process device/browser
  information and approximate location derived from technical request data.

2) How We Use Information
- To authenticate users and maintain guest/account sessions.
- To save and sync scores, attempts, and profile progress.
- To display leaderboard results.
- To protect score integrity and reduce abuse.
- To deliver the Web app, secure requests, troubleshoot and maintain reliability.
- With your Web Analytics consent, to understand visits, quiz usage and completion
  and identify problems in the player journey. Optional measurement is separate
  from the services needed to sign in, play and save scores.

Legal bases under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):
- Providing the service you request, including authentication, score history,
  synchronization and the account leaderboard: performance of the service
  agreement (Article 6(1)(b)), limited to data necessary for those features.
- Essential security, abuse prevention and reliability: legitimate interests
  (Article 6(1)(f)) in protecting the service and its users. These interests must
  be balanced against your rights; you can object as described below.
- Optional audience measurement: your separate consent (Article 6(1)(a) and
  French cookie rules). It is not required to play or accepted through the Terms.
- Handling GDPR rights requests and any legally required records: compliance
  with the applicable legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Other support needed
  to provide your requested service uses Article 6(1)(b).

3) Data Storage And Providers
- Vercel hosts and delivers the Web app. Requests for pages and files expose
  technical connection/request information to the hosting provider for delivery,
  operation and security.
- Google Firebase Authentication manages guest and linked-account sessions.
- Google Cloud Firestore stores account/profile, gameplay and leaderboard data.
- Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) provides optional Web audience measurement
  after acceptance. Advertising signals and personalization remain disabled.
- The browser also requests application libraries from Google-hosted Firebase
  services. Refusing Analytics does not prevent the hosting, authentication,
  database and library requests needed to operate the app.
- The default Firestore database for this app is configured in eur3 (European
  multi-region). This does not locate every provider service in Europe: Google
  documents that Firebase Authentication operates from United States data centers.
  Vercel delivery and optional Analytics can also involve international processing.
- Applicable provider terms and transfer mechanisms depend on the service and
  configuration; information about them can be requested at the support address.
  Google's Firebase privacy information is at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  and Vercel's notice is at https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice.
- Refusing Analytics does not change the account/score data needed in Firebase
  or the technical requests needed to deliver the app.

- Email addresses and authentication credentials are not leaderboard fields.
  Firebase manages authentication credentials; the app does not copy passwords
  into Firestore profile or gameplay documents.

4) Anonymous Guest Accounts
- You can use Quiznetic as a guest.
- Guest data is tied to your Firebase UID. This is a pseudonymous identifier;
  guest mode is not a guarantee of anonymous data under the GDPR.
- If you upgrade your account, data may remain associated with the same UID.

5) Data Sharing
- We do not sell your personal data.
- Account leaderboard entries expose the public display name, score, quiz
  category/difficulty and update time to authenticated users, including guests.
  Choose a nickname that you are comfortable sharing. New writes use Player
  when no account display name was chosen; existing names are not automatically
  renamed. Guest scores are not published as guest leaderboard entries.
- We may share data with service providers only as needed to run Quiznetic.
- We may disclose data when required by law or to enforce app security.

6) Data Retention
- Account/profile, score and attempt records are used to maintain your account,
  score history, synchronization and leaderboard participation while those
  features are provided. Verified account deletion removes these active records;
  merely signing out does not request deletion of a permanent account.
- There is currently no automatic purge of inactive accounts or old attempts.
  No fixed automatic inactivity-deletion period is promised by this policy.
- A completed deletion leaves only a minimal technical lock (UID path, status
  and timestamps) for at least 65 minutes to block still-valid tokens. An
  operator then verifies absence of the account/data and removes that lock.
- Support material is limited to what is needed to handle the request. Any
  separate retention for a legal obligation or legal claims must be limited to
  the necessary records and period; it does not justify keeping gameplay data
  indefinitely.
- Provider logs, backup copies and earlier Analytics data have separate retention
  and erasure processes. The 180-day browser consent choice is not a retention
  period for account records, GA4 events, provider logs or backups.

7) Your Rights And Account Deletion
- You may request access, correction, erasure or restriction of your personal
  data, portability where applicable, and object to processing based on
  legitimate interests. You can withdraw optional Analytics consent at any time
  without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.
- Send requests to quizneticapp@gmail.com. For account deletion, a useful subject
  is "Quiznetic - account and data deletion". Where possible, write from the
  account email and indicate the account/sign-in method. Guest users can contact
  support while their session is still accessible. A UID or public nickname
  alone does not establish ownership.
- Do not send passwords, session/refresh tokens or a service-account key. We may
  request proportionate additional evidence if there is reasonable doubt about
  identity; an identity-document copy is not required by default.
- We respond without undue delay and within one month of receiving a request.
  If complexity or the number of requests requires up to two additional months,
  we explain the extension and its reasons within the first month. If a legal
  exception prevents part of the requested action, we explain the reason and
  the available complaint options within that period.
- Following ownership verification, the operator deletion procedure disables
  the account, revokes refresh tokens, removes its Auth record, recursively
  deletes its private profile/scores/attempts and removes its leaderboard entries.
  This deletion is irreversible for those active account records.
- This operation cannot remotely erase site data on your devices. Clear Quiznetic
  cookies/site storage on every device to remove local scores/preferences and
  pending synchronization. Guest data may be impossible to reconnect to you after
  you lose the session; support will explain what can be identified safely.
- Earlier GA4 records and provider logs/backups are handled separately, using
  applicable provider mechanisms and legal retention limits. An email address
  alone may not identify pseudonymous Analytics records. We do not promise
  immediate erasure of every provider copy merely because Auth was deleted.
- You may lodge a complaint with the French supervisory authority, CNIL:
  https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes. Other competent supervisory authorities and
  judicial remedies remain available where applicable.

8) Children
- The Terms require parental/guardian permission for use by minors. This is
  separate from consent to optional data processing.
- In France, for online-service processing based on consent, children under 15
  require joint consent with the holder of parental responsibility. Age 15 is
  not a blanket minimum age for every feature or every legal basis.
- No verified parental-consent workflow is currently provided by this MVP.
  Contact support about a child's data or its erasure; the absence of that
  workflow is not a claim that parental consent has been verified.

9) Security

  storage or transmission is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

10) Policy Updates
- We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

12) Web Audience Measurement Preferences
- On the Web, optional audience measurement uses Google Analytics for Firebase.
- Analytics is off on a new browser visit until you choose Accept analytics.
  A valid acceptance saved in that browser restores your choice on later visits.
  Refusing does not affect accounts, gameplay or scores. Advertising signals and
  personalization stay off.
- After acceptance, Analytics may store first-party cookies and measure visits,
  screen views, quiz category/difficulty, completion and score-submission events.
- Acceptance or refusal is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. After that,
  a new choice is requested. It is separate from account sign-in and from
  acceptance of the Terms of Service. Clearing browser storage may reset it.
- Use Analytics preferences on any screen to change your choice. Refusing after
  acceptance stops future collection, removes accessible first-party GA cookies
  and reloads the page. Changing preferences during a quiz can end that session.
- Withdrawal does not automatically erase previously collected data. Contact
  quizneticapp@gmail.com for questions or requests about your data.

13) Cookies And Browser Storage
- Functional browser storage supports your authentication session, preferences,
  saved local scores and pending score synchronization. It is separate from
  optional Analytics and is not disabled by refusing audience measurement.
- We store the Analytics choice and its timestamp locally so that we can respect
  your preference without requiring an account. This preference record is not
  an advertising identifier.
- After Analytics acceptance, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such
  as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish browsers and maintain measurement session state.
  Cookie lifetime depends on the service/browser settings; the 180-day duration
  above applies to your consent choice, not to all cookies or server-side data.
- Before you choose, or if you refuse, the app does not load its Analytics SDK or
  send optional Analytics events. Gameplay, account and hosting requests still
  occur when needed to provide the service.
- You can also manage site cookies and storage in your browser. Clearing all site
  data may sign you out or remove local preferences and unsynchronized scores.

14) Ad-Free Web MVP
- This Web MVP does not display advertising or offer in-app purchases.
- Optional Analytics is used for product audience measurement, not to enable
  advertising personalization. Refusal does not restrict access to the quizzes.
